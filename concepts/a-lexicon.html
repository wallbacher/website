κλέος

kléos
/ˈkle.os/ · n., Ancient Greek

Glory. Renown. Literally “what is heard”: the story people tell about you, long after you have left the room.

Etymology · tap a word

One root, to hear. Every hero in the old songs had a kleos of their own: a different deed, a different story, told their own way. Achilles traded a long life for kléos áphthiton, glory that never fades.
